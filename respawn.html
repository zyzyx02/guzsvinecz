RESPAWN Flow Diagram GeneratorRESPAWN folyamatábra-generátor

Player reviews are not surveys. Every corpus passes through a review-generating process before analysis. Enter your counts and settings below, check that the numbers add up, and download the diagram for your methods section.

A játékos-review nem kérdőív. Minden korpusz átmegy egy review-generáló folyamaton, mielőtt elemzés lesz belőle. Add meg alább a számaidat és beállításaidat, ellenőrizd, hogy a számok összeadódnak-e, majd töltsd le az ábrát a módszertani részhez.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; a draft is kept only in this browser so you can return to it.

Minden a böngésződben fut. Semmit nem küldünk el sehova; a piszkozat csak ebben a böngészőben marad meg, hogy később folytathasd.

The RESPAWN checklistA RESPAWN checklist

    
Seven items, one for each stage of the review-generating process. The flow diagram covers the counts; the checklist covers what to state in the text.

    
Hét tétel, a review-generáló folyamat minden szakaszára egy. A folyamatábra a darabszámokat mutatja; a checklist azt, amit a szövegben kell leírni.

    

      
ItemTételReport at minimumMinimálisan közlendő

      
        
R · Reviewer selectionWhich reviews were eligible (purchase type, free copies, Early Access); playtime-at-review distribution; any reviewer-level filters; treatment of edited reviews

        
E · Events and platform effectsTime window; known events inside it (releases, updates, sales, review bombs); whether flagged off-topic periods were included; sort order

        
S · Sampling and collectionSource and endpoint; every parameter, including defaults left unchanged; date of collection; number retrieved vs. number reported by the platform

        
P · PreprocessingEach filter (length, language, duplicates, non-text content) and the number of reviews it removed

        
A · Analytic and measurement choicesTool or model and version; label set or taxonomy; thresholds and hyperparameters; validation against human judgement on comparable text; robustness checks

        
W · Wording and driftLanguages included and how they were detected; handling of slang and game-specific vocabulary; stability checks for multi-year corpora

        
N · Norms for ethics and data sharingTerms of collection; handling of identifiers; verbatim vs. paraphrased quotations; what is shared (data, IDs, code) and where
